# Phase 5 Plan 01: Multi-Language & Regional Idiom Adaptation

## Tasks

1. **Site-Aware Language & Country Resolution (`LANG-01`)**:
   - Enrich `FLEET_SITE_PROFILES` in `src/katteb/wordpress.py` with `default_language`, `default_country`, and `locale_region`.
   - Implement `normalize_language_name()` and `resolve_site_language_and_country()`.
   - Expose `language` and `localize_idioms` on `WordPressFleetManager.expand_and_update_post()`, `wp expand-post`, `wp batch-expand`, `pipeline-dispatch`, and `PipelineEventPayload`.

2. **Regional Idiom & Cultural Prompt Adaptation (`LANG-02`)**:
   - Define `LOCALIZATION_PROFILES` for `de`, `gr`, `es`, `fr`, `nl`, `it`, and `us`/`gb`.
   - Implement `build_localized_guidelines()` to inject native terminology, local transit/currency conventions, and cultural ethics rules without exceeding 2,000 characters.

3. **Multi-Language Quality & Cultural Guardrails (`LANG-03`)**:
   - Extend `validate_generated_content()` with multilingual AI-refusal detection (`als ki-sprachmodell`, `como modelo de lenguaje`, `en tant que modèle de langage`, `ως γλωσσικό μοντέλο`, etc.) and Greece donkey transportation exclusion.
